Issue body actions

There's apparently been an attack on the openconnect-sso package. This time putting a dropper in the aur-repo and the build() phase.
It appears that the matching offending commit has been reverted in AUR.

Unfortunately there are no real stable indicators to scan for. The matching offending binary however still seems to be available in AUR as deeplink.

Reports:

  • https://lists.archlinux.org/archives/list/aur-general@lists.archlinux.org/thread/PR77K3SB6RFSTYP3KYOJOOX56SMXGBWO/
  • https://lists.archlinux.org/archives/list/aur-general@lists.archlinux.org/thread/PMATLG676MPBWUJPZZ75WJ4H3BKE7TVL/

Affected package:

  • https://aur.archlinux.org/packages/openconnect-sso
  • Brief analysis attempt with Claude by an AUR user: https://claude.ai/share/d8111b00-156c-405b-9faf-85631176092b
  • Offending commit: https://aur.archlinux.org/cgit/aur.git/commit/?h=openconnect-sso&id=9d107786fa851c6305dadb3573aab5330a041524

Since there's not much of real reliable value to be done here, I'll just leave this up for reference and warning.
