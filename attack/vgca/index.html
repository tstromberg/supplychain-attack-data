Synopsis

Operation SignSight replaced official VGCA signing-toolkit MSI installers. The packages installed the legitimate app and the PhantomNet espionage backdoor.

Story

Vietnam's Government Certification Authority distributes software that organizations use for digital signatures. In 2020, attackers compromised the VGCA website and replaced the official x86 and x64 MSI installers for version 8.3.

The installers still delivered the expected signing toolkit. They also dropped a modified `eToken.exe` and a backdoor ESET named PhantomNet. That made the compromise hard to spot: the product installed, the workflow continued, and the implant started beside it.

ESET placed the malicious installers on the VGCA site between 2020-07-23 and 2020-08-16. Victim telemetry pointed to government and private-sector organizations in Vietnam, consistent with espionage rather than bulk monetization.

VGCA said it notified affected users and restored clean installers. This record tracks the official website distribution of the MSI packages, not the certificate authority's cryptographic root trust.
